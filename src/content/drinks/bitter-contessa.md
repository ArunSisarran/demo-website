Our house negroni, stirred long over one block of ice and left to open up in the
glass. Equal parts gin, red vermouth, and a bitter aperitivo we macerate in-house
with orange peel and gentian root for eleven days.

It arrives cold and closed. Give it four minutes and it turns into something rounder
and quietly sweet — which is the whole reason we serve it in a heavy tumbler rather
than a coupe.
