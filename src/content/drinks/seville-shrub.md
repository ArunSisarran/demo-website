A drinking vinegar, which sounds worse than it is. Seville oranges macerated in
demerara for a week, then let down with apple cider vinegar and topped with soda at
the pass.

It does what a good aperitif does — resets the palate and makes you hungry — with
nothing in it. The most ordered drink on the list before seven o’clock.
