A pétillant naturel from a two-hectare plot in the Loire, bottled before primary
fermentation finished and left to sort itself out under a crown cap. No filtration,
no dosage, nothing added.

Expect a little sediment and a soft, bready fizz rather than champagne’s fine bead.
Served by the glass because a bottle rarely survives the table.
